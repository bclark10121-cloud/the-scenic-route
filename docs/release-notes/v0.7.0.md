# The Scenic Route v0.7.0

Release 7: the standards review pieces every game now shares. A Records page, button remapping with Hold or Toggle for Gas, backup codes, a picture setting and steadier music levels.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.0. Your saves stay.
2. Press Play, pick your slot and drive for a minute.
3. Open the Menu. Press Records. Your 15 achievements are there with any you already earned marked Earned, then your stats: finds, biomes, laps, longest jump, longest drift, top speed and miles.
4. Open Settings, then Change controls. Pick Handbrake and press another key or button. If another action had it, the two swap. Set Gas to Toggle and drive. Reset controls puts everything back.
5. In Settings, try Picture Low, Medium and High, and Battery saver.
6. Open the slot list. Press Copy code on your slot. Paste it into the box under the slots on another device and press Load code.
7. Listen to the music. It should sound about as loud as before.

## New in this version

- Records in the Menu: your 15 achievements and 7 stats on one page (G4.10, G4.11).
- Change controls in Settings for steering, gas, brake, handbrake, reset, camera, auto drive and the journal. Gas can be Hold or Toggle (G5.22, G5.23).
- Picture (Low, Medium, High) and Battery saver in Settings (G6.12).
- Backup codes in the slot list: Copy code and Load code (G5.19).
- Old saves are now tested every release (G5.24).
- An error log on the device. Copy bug report in Tester tools adds the last ten errors (G5.27).
- Music and sounds brought to one loudness level (G5.28), with the music target raised to keep the same mix.
