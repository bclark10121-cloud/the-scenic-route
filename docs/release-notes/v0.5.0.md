# The Scenic Route v0.5.0

## New in this version

- Coast Road: a sixth biome on the west edge. Pale sand, palms, a tarmac road along the shore and the open sea.
- A seaside lap circuit, its own song and 32 new finds. The total is 198.
- Drive into the sea and you are put back on the road, as with the lakes.
- All 32 new item pictures come from the Patch.gg library (shells, fish, seaweed and more).

## Test checklist

1. Drive west on the main loop and take the coast road. Does the biome name show and the song change?
2. Do the palms and the sea look right from the far camera?
3. Do the new sea-themed pictures look right in the journal?
4. Any lag now that the world has six biomes?
