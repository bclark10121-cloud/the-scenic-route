# The Scenic Route v0.4.0

## New in this version

- Autumn Woods: a fifth biome in the east with gold and red trees, its own song and its own lap circuit. 33 new finds bring the total to 166.
- Broken bridges: four ramp pairs with a gap between them. Clear the gap for the "Mind the Gap" achievement.
- Achievements: 15 to earn. The journal lists the ones you have and the ones left.
- Christmas theme: a button (or key X) turns on snowfall, a Santa hat on the car and red and green glows.
- 12 of the new item pictures come from the Patch.gg library.

## Test checklist

1. Drive east on the main loop into Autumn Woods. Does the song change?
2. Find a broken bridge (a ramp with railings and a second ramp facing it). Clear the gap at speed.
3. Open the journal. Do achievements show?
4. Turn the Christmas theme on and off.
5. Do the new item pictures look right next to the older ones?
