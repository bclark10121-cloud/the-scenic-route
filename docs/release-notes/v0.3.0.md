# The Scenic Route v0.3.0

## New in this version

- Red Desert: a fourth biome with a dirt road, sand, cactus and its own song. 33 new finds bring the total to 133.
- Ramps beside the main roads, with cones to knock over. The game measures each jump and keeps your best.
- Lap circuits: one in every biome. Drive through the red and cream gate to start the clock. Your best lap is saved.
- Records in the journal: best laps, best jump, longest drift, top speed and miles driven.
- Bridges with plank decks and railings where roads cross water.
- Road works: cones, barrier boards and a dug-up stretch. Everything can be knocked over.
- Drift button (was Hand): the handbrake now swings the car round much harder. It also works with auto drive on.
- Road fixes: no more flicker or stray shards where roads meet, and road colours blend between biomes.
- The surface label shows the road when you are half on it.

## Test checklist

1. Road junctions on your phone: is the flicker gone?
2. Hold Drift while turning at speed. Does the back swing out?
3. Hit a ramp at speed. Do you see "Jump N ft"?
4. Drive through a circuit gate, go round and come back through. Do you get a lap time?
5. Open the journal. Do your records show?
6. Find the Red Desert (south). Does the music change?
7. Cross a bridge and drive through road works.
