# The Scenic Route v0.5.3

A standards release. No new roads or biomes. The same Settings, save slots, menus and tester tools as every other game, full Xbox controller play and a new app icon.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.5.2. The new icon shows a red rally car on a winding road. Open the game. The title card says Version 0.5.3 and has three buttons: Start driving, Slot 1 and Settings.
2. Your finds, laps and records from v0.5.2 are still there. They are now slot 1.
3. Open Settings. Music is first. Press it: Full, Off, Low, Medium, High, Full. Below the standard switches sit Auto drive, Camera, Handling and Christmas theme. Nothing asks you to save.
4. Start driving. Press Menu at the top right. The car stops where it is. The Menu reads Resume, Settings, How to play, Journal, Reset car, Title. Press Resume and the drive goes on.
5. With an Xbox controller: press View. The Menu opens. Move with the D-pad, choose with A, go back with B. D-pad up opens the journal.
6. Turn the controller off mid-drive. The Menu opens and a line says the controller disconnected.
7. On the title card press Slot 1, then Slot 2. The world rebuilds with nothing found. Press Slot 2 again and switch back to slot 1: your finds are back.

## New in this version

- Settings, the same in every game: Music and Sound in five levels, Full screen in a browser, Frame rate, Rumble, then Auto drive, Camera, Handling, Christmas theme and Controls, then Text size and Reset settings. Every change saves by itself.
- Five save slots on the title card. Each keeps its own finds, achievements, laps and records. Delete a slot with two presses.
- Settings are kept apart from the slots, so deleting a slot never changes them.
- The game pauses while the Menu, the journal, How to play, Settings or the slot list is open, when the app loses focus and when the controller disconnects.
- The Menu reads Resume, Settings, How to play, Journal, Reset car, Title. Title takes you back to the title card and its save slots.
- How to play: what the game is and every control for keyboard, Xbox controller and touch.
- Full Xbox controller play. Every button on the title card and in every menu works with the D-pad and A. View and Menu open the Menu. The journal moved to D-pad up. The journal and How to play scroll with the D-pad or the right stick.
- The title card shows the controls for the device you used last.
- Rumble: big jumps and every find buzz the phone and rumble the controller. Turn it off in Settings.
- A new app icon of its own. It used to share the lantern from another game. The Android icon fits round and square shapes.
- Tester tools behind a code at the bottom of Settings (test builds only): go to any biome, circuit, bridge or road works, collect letters and finds, clear records, reset or fill the save, and Copy bug report.
- A privacy page. The game collects no data and the APK does not ask for the internet permission.
- The source moved to a private repo. This repo keeps the game page, the builds, the release notes and the backlog.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.8.1.

## Install

v0.5.3 installs over v0.5.2 and earlier. Your finds, laps and records become slot 1. Your sound, music, camera, handling, auto drive and Christmas choices carry over into Settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Settings fits the screen on a phone, a tablet and a desktop browser window. The list scrolls.
2. Set Text size to Larger. The title card and Settings still fit. Set it back to Normal.
3. Sound at Off silences the engine and finds. Music at Off silences the music. Low, Medium and High are quieter steps.
4. Rumble at Off stops the buzz on a big jump.
5. Keys C, H, G, M and X still change camera, handling, auto drive, music and Christmas, and Settings shows the change.
6. Start a lap, open the Menu for a while, then Resume. The lap clock did not run while the Menu was open.
7. The Menu's Title saves and goes back to the title card. Start driving picks up where you were.
8. Delete the slot you are playing. The game moves to another slot and rebuilds the world for it.
9. Tester tools: Settings, Tester tools, enter your code. Try Golden Hills, Start of a circuit, Collect the next letter and Copy bug report.
10. The Android back button closes the top panel, or opens the Menu while driving.

## Not tested

A real phone, a real controller and sound. The checks ran headless and in a headless browser.
