# The Scenic Route v0.8.3

The Scenic Route now runs on Godot 4.7.2 (G6.19). Nothing about play changes. Your drive feel and jumps notes from the playtest sweep come in their own release once you say what felt off.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.2. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game. The title track plays and the start screen shows.
3. Play for a couple of minutes with touch, then with the Xbox controller.
4. Open Settings and the Tester tools. Both open as before.
