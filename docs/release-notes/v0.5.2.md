# The Scenic Route v0.5.2

A standards release. The repo now carries `STANDARDS.md`, the Godot 3D engineering standards for browser and Android, and `STANDARDS_OVERRIDES.md`, the list of places this game differs and why. This version closes the gaps that affect play on a phone.

## New in this version

- Safe areas: the speed readout, menu, messages and touch pads keep clear of camera notches, rounded corners and gesture bars.
- Safer saves: on Android the save is written to a spare file and swapped in. The game also saves when the app is paused, loses focus or closes. In the browser each save is sent to the browser store at once.
- Smoother motion on 90 and 120 Hz screens (physics interpolation).
- Phones hold 60 frames a second. Phones and browsers draw the 3D view at 85% size to save heat and battery.
- Touch pads hide when you use a keyboard or gamepad and come back on a touch.
- The Android back button opens and closes the menu, or closes the journal.
- Buttons are a little taller (48 px).
- Fix: the journal no longer runs off the right side of the screen. Records and pictures scroll together and the Close button is always in view.

## Test checklist

1. On a phone with a notch or rounded corners: is every HUD item fully visible in landscape, both ways round?
2. Does the picture look softer than before? The 3D view is drawn at 85% size. Say so if it looks blurry.
3. Does the car and camera motion look smooth, with no judder or rubber-banding after a reset?
4. Retroid or gamepad: touch the screen, then press a button. Do the touch pads disappear and come back on the next touch?
5. Press the Android back button while driving. Does the menu open? With the journal open, does it close the journal?
6. Change a setting, swipe the app away and open it again. Is the setting kept?
7. Open the journal. Is the Close button visible and does the list scroll?
