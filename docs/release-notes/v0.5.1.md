# The Scenic Route v0.5.1

## New in this version

- Roads join cleanly. A joining road now tucks in under the main road, so the blunt grey ends at junctions are gone.
- Railings keep well clear of junctions.
- Grass, sand and deep snow no longer slow the car. Top speed off road is now within 3 mph of gravel, so run-ups to ramps and jumps work from any direction.
- The car now rights itself in the air as well as on the ground, so fast off-road jumps land on the wheels.
- Auto drive keeps more speed off road.
- Backlog: a quality of life review (items 25 to 59) and the startup menu request.

## Test checklist

1. Drive through three or four junctions. Do the roads join with no blunt ends, flicker or stray railings?
2. Leave the road on grass, sand and deep snow. Does the car keep its speed?
3. Take a ramp from an off-road run-up. Is the jump longer than before, and does the car land on its wheels?
4. Does off-road driving now feel too fast or too slippery anywhere?
