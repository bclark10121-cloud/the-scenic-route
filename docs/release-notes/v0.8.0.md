# The Scenic Route v0.8.0

Drive holding your phone upright or sideways. Back now sits at the top left of every menu screen, clear of Android's bottom bar.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.7.0. Your saves stay.
2. Hold the phone upright and open the game. The start screen fits the tall screen.
3. Tap Settings. Back sits at the top left. The list scrolls under it. Tap Back.
4. Tap Play, pick your slot and drive upright for a minute. The car keeps a good size and more road shows ahead.
5. Open the Menu. Upright, its buttons sit under the speed readout.
6. Open How to play, then the Journal. Each has Back at the top left.
7. Turn the phone sideways mid-drive. The drive keeps going and the view switches.

## New in this version

- Full play upright and sideways, on Android and in the browser. The game turns with your phone.
- Upright: a camera with a narrower side view so the car stays a good size, the Menu row under the speed readout and the lap clock under it.
- Back at the top left of Settings, the slot list, How to play and the Journal. Close at the top right is gone.
- Touch steering reaches as far upright as sideways.
- Turning the phone mid-drive keeps your drive. Nothing reloads.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0: the shared header bar with Back at the top and the screen fit for both orientations.

## Install

Version 0.8.0 installs over 0.7.0 and keeps your saves. The game follows your phone's rotation lock. Turn the lock off to drive upright.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: the new five-size browser check with a turn mid-drive, the menu, safe area, driving, review and sound browser checks, the find reach check and the save, Settings, device and Tester tools checks. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone, a real tablet or the Retroid. The handling and road bots were not rerun: the car and roads did not change.
