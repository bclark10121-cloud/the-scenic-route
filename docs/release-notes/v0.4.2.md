# The Scenic Route v0.4.2

## Changed

- One Menu button at the top right. Tap it and the options open in a row across the top. Tap Close to hide them. Esc on a keyboard and Back on a controller do the same.

- A game standards handoff zip now ships with every release: standards, templates and reusable code for new games.

## Test checklist

1. Is only the Menu button showing while you drive?
2. Tap Menu. Do all the options show across the top?
3. Tap Close. Do they hide again?

---

## From v0.4.1

## Fixed (from BC's playtest screenshots)

- Roads: no more slivers down the middle of a road or broken patches where roads meet. Each road is now its own raised bed with a soft verge, and the ground under it sits lower.
- Railings on bridges are solid. They stop at junctions so they never block a road.
- Trees, rocks, bushes and cactus are solid out to their branches. You bump into them instead of driving through.

## Test checklist

1. Drive through the junction from your screenshot. Are the broken patches gone?
2. Drive a long straight. Are the slivers in the road gone?
3. Push the car against a bridge railing, a tree, a rock and a bush. Does each one stop you?
4. Leave the road and come back on. Does the small drop at the road edge feel fine?
