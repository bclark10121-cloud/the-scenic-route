# The Scenic Route v0.5.4

A sound fix. In the browser the game played no music and no sound effects (recent versions). Now you hear the music for each biome and the pickup and letter chimes. Nothing else changed.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page on a computer and press Play in the browser. Click once on the game. The title music starts.
2. Open Settings. Set Music to Low. The music gets quieter. Set it back to Full.
3. Start a drive. The biome music plays. Drive through a pickup: you hear the chime.
4. Settings, Sound to Off. The effects stop and the music keeps playing. Set Sound back to Full.
5. Install the APK over v0.5.3. Sound works on Android as before.

## New in this version

- Sound works in the browser again. The game made its Music and Sfx sound channels in code after it started. In a browser build, Godot does not connect channels made that way to the speakers, so everything sent to them was silent. The channels now come from a project file that loads at startup, the same fix Home by Dusk got on 2026-11-15.
- A new browser sound test listens to what the page really plays and fails when it hears nothing. It runs before every release from now on.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.8.2. No kit change.

## Install

Version 0.5.4 installs over 0.5.3 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser: the title music plays after the first click or key.
2. Browser: Music and Sound in Settings change the level, and Off silences each one on its own.
3. Browser: music changes between areas, sound effects play during play.
4. Android: music and sound play as before.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried.
- Sound was measured in headless Chrome by a script that reads the page's audio output. Nobody listened by ear.
- The APK was built, signed and checked, but never installed. Android was not affected by this bug: the same build plays music when run by the Godot engine directly.
