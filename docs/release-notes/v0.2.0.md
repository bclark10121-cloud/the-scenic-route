# The Scenic Route v0.2.0

## New in this version

- Three biomes on one map: Pine Lakes, Golden Hills and Snow Pass.
- A 3 km main loop and two roads across the middle.
- Lakes. A swim puts the car back on the road.
- 100 things to find: the letters S-C-E-N-I-C and 94 keepsakes.
- Journal, find counter and a save file.
- Music, one track per biome.
- Auto drive: the car works the pedals and you only steer.
- Camera sits higher and farther back.
- First Android APK.

## Test checklist

1. Drive the full loop. Does each biome name appear as you cross over?
2. Turn on auto drive (G or B). Does it slow for bends?
3. Pick up a keepsake and a letter. Do you hear a chime and see the message?
4. Open the journal (J). Does the find show?
5. Close and reopen the game. Are your finds still there?
6. Drive into a lake. Are you put back on the road?
