# The Scenic Route v0.9.0

Race your own best lap. A see-through ghost car now drives your best lap on each circuit beside you.

## Five minute test

1. Drive to any circuit's start gate (two red posts and a banner) and drive through it. "Lap started" shows.
2. Drive one full lap round the circuit and back through the gate. Your lap time shows, then "Ghost saved: race your best lap next time".
3. Keep going into the next lap. A pale see-through car drives your last lap beside you, from the gate.
4. Beat it and your new lap becomes the ghost. Lose to it and the old ghost stays.
5. Settings > Ghost car turns it off. The car itself should drive exactly like 0.8.3: same grip, jumps, drifts and camera.

## What changed

New
- Ghost car of your best lap on each circuit (backlog item 48). It is your own run only, never a rival (G4.4). Each slot keeps its own ghosts in small files beside the save.
- Settings > Ghost car (your best lap), on by default.
- Tester tools > Clear lap times and records also clears the ghosts.

Under the hood
- The car's handling, the chase camera, jump and drift lengths and lap timing now come from the shared studio kit (kit 1.21.0, blocks K5 and K11). The kit was pulled out of this game, so nothing about the drive changes.
- No 60 frame cap on phones any more (G6.20). Battery saver in Settings still caps at 30.
- From the newer kit: full screen by default in the browser (G5.11) and fixes to the talk box and settings.

Fixed
- The start screen and Tester tools showed version 0.8.2 in 0.8.3. They now show 0.9.0.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.21.0 (was 1.15.0). Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Installs over 0.8.3 and keeps your saves.

## Full test checklist

1. Same drive as 0.8.3: the 0.8.3 car and the 0.9.0 car on one scripted drive (tarmac, gravel, sand, a handbrake turn, a 1.5 second jump): 1,090 physics steps, every position the same. The handling bot's numbers for every surface and handling choice match 0.8.3, every car stays upright (up_min 1.00).
2. Lap timing: every circuit (track 0 to 5) times a lap. Tracks 0 and 1 give the same lap times as 0.8.3 to the hundredth.
3. Ghost: a two lap run on the Pine Lakes circuit. The ghost showed for the whole second lap, on average 6 m from the car, and its file was saved.
4. Jumps: all 15 ramps, rolled 0, the same as 0.8.3.
5. REACH (198 finds, bad 0), DEVICE, SAVESTORE, SETTINGS_SLOTS, DEBUGMENU, COMMON and SECRETS checks.
6. Browser: the standards test (title, Settings, slots, Menu, pretend Xbox controller), the driving test, the orientation test at five sizes and the 1080p and wide screen check. All with errors [].
7. The APK is signed with the studio key (signature starts 5db69ffb) and asks for no internet.
